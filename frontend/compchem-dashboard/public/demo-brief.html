A useful 10-minute walkthrough

Keep the conversation on decisions, not navigation.

  1. Set the question.Start with the decision the team needs to make: nominate a lead, compare conditions, or review a delivery.
  2. Trace the record.Move from the campaign into its source runs, samples, or molecules and inspect the captured context.
  3. Review the checks.Show QC results where they appear in the work—not as a separate spreadsheet or afterthought.
  4. Close the evidence loop.Open the audit history, verify the delivery, and export the record for a reviewer.

Questions worth asking

Use the product to answer these in the room.

Where did this decision come from?

Trace the campaign’s material inputs, applied methods, QC findings, and linked record of events.

What changed between conditions or candidates?

Use the comparison views to inspect metrics alongside the underlying experimental or computational context.

Can a reviewer independently check this later?

Audit trails, source references, verification results, and downloadable evidence packages are available from the same workflow.
